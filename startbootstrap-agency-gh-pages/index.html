About Us

How it all began...

  • 2009-2011

    Our Humble Beginnings

    Every great story starts with a single frame. For us, it began with a borrowed camera, a passion for capturing moments, and a deep belief that memories deserve to be preserved with heart and honesty. Our journey started in a small corner of a living room, where natural light was our best friend and creativity was our biggest asset. With each shutter click, we weren’t just taking photos—we were learning, growing, and dreaming of what could be. There were no fancy studios or high-end gear in the beginning—just a vision, a relentless commitment to excellence, and a desire to make people feel seen, valued, and remembered through our lens. Today, Mustard Studion stands not only as a photography studio, but as a testament to what faith, hard work, and consistency can build. We’re still growing, still learning, and still capturing the magic in everyday life. And this is only the beginning.

  • March 2011

    A Studio is Born

    There were no grand setups—just natural light, a blank wall, and a heart full of vision. Each shoot was a lesson. Each client, a stepping stone. We photographed birthdays, portraits, events—anything that allowed us to tell stories through the lens and serve people with what we had. Over the years, we’ve grown—step by step, frame by frame. From that tiny space in 2011 to where we are today, our journey has been marked by grace, growth, and gratitude. At Mustard Studio, we still carry that same spirit of humility and creativity. Because no matter how far we go, we never forget where we started.

  • December 2015

    Transition to Full Service

    As time went on, so did our vision. With consistent work, unwavering belief, and the support of our clients, we gradually evolved. What began as a modest side venture has grown into a full-service photography studio, offering everything from studio sessions and event coverage to professional editing, printing, and visual branding solutions. Today, Mustard Studio stands as a creative space where vision meets excellence. We’ve expanded our services, invested in top-tier equipment, built a strong creative team, and most importantly—remained grounded in the values that started it all: passion, purpose, and people.

  • July 2020

    Phase Two Expansion

    This next chapter marks a bold expansion of Mustard Studio — not just in space, but in vision. We are redesigning our studio to include: Multiple themed shooting spaces, a dedicated editing and client preview lounge, creative training hub for aspiring photographers and a content creation suite for brands and influencers.
